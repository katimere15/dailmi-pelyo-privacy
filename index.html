Summary

Pelyo is a local-first spending pace and budget companion. Budget plans, check-ins, categories, and legacy ledger records stay on the device unless the user explicitly exports a file. Pelyo has no account sign-in, bank connection, or cloud synchronization. Google services are used for limited advertising and optional crash diagnostics as described below.

1. Information stored on your device

Pelyo stores information you enter or create in the app’s private storage, including:

  • monthly everyday-spending plans, category allocations, currencies, week-start preferences, check-in dates and amounts, pace snapshots, monthly approvals, and closing status;
  • custom categories, language and display preferences, reminder settings, and widget settings;
  • when legacy ledger features are used, account or card nicknames, balances, transaction dates, amounts, categories, notes, budgets, recurring items, and deleted-item history; and
  • temporary encrypted recovery copies and recreatable widget or reminder cache needed to operate the app safely.

This information is used to calculate budgets, remaining amounts, spending pace, references, statistics, reminders, and widgets. Dailmi does not operate a server that receives these financial records. Pelyo does not require an account, connect to a financial institution, or synchronize records to a Pelyo cloud service.

2. Files you choose to export or import

Encrypted backup

You can create a password-protected .mnb backup and choose where to save it. Encryption is performed on the device. Pelyo does not store or transmit the backup password, and Dailmi cannot recover a lost password. If you choose a cloud or third-party document provider, that provider processes the file under its own policy.

CSV export

You can export selected ledger records as an unencrypted CSV file. The file can contain dates, transaction types, amounts, categories, account or card names, and notes. You select the destination or sharing recipient. Dailmi does not receive the exported file. External backup and CSV files must be deleted by you from their saved locations.

3. Advertising and consent choices

Pelyo uses Google Mobile Ads and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). The app places one 300×250 MREC ad after the content on Statistics and one adaptive banner after the content on More. It does not place ads on Today, Check-in, Plan, onboarding, widgets, notifications, app launch or exit, or CSV completion. Pelyo requests non-personalized ads only after the Google SDK reports that an ad request is permitted. If consent is unavailable, denied where applicable, the device is offline, or an ad fails to load, the app keeps the feature available and removes the empty ad space.

Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share IP address (which can estimate general location), product interactions, diagnostic information, and device or account identifiers for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention. Data is encrypted in transit. Pelyo does not add budget amounts, check-ins, category names, account names, notes, exported file names, or internal financial identifiers to ad requests.

Where Google requires a privacy-options entry point, Pelyo displays it in Privacy & app info so you can revisit advertising choices. Android advertising controls may also let you reset or delete the advertising ID.

4. Optional crash diagnostics

Pelyo includes Firebase Core and Firebase Crashlytics. Crash reporting is off by default and can be enabled or disabled in Privacy & app info. Core budget features work without enabling diagnostics. When enabled, Crashlytics may process installation identifiers, crash traces, app version, device model and architecture, operating-system information, timestamps, and related technical diagnostics.

Pelyo’s own non-fatal reports use fixed diagnostic codes and sanitized stacks. The app does not intentionally attach financial records, amounts, category or account names, notes, SQL values, file contents, or raw exception messages. Turning diagnostics off stops new app reports and requests deletion of unsent reports; reports already sent or in transit cannot be immediately withdrawn from the device. Google states that Crashlytics begins removing crash traces and associated identifiers from live and backup systems after 90 days.

Firebase Analytics is not included and analytics collection is disabled.

5. Reminders, widgets, permissions, and device backup

If you enable reminders, Pelyo asks for notification permission on supported Android versions and schedules notifications locally using device time. It uses boot and time-change events to restore schedules; it does not use a remote push-notification service. Notification text does not include an amount. Widget amounts are hidden by default and appear only if you choose to show them.

Internet and network-state access support ads, consent, and optional diagnostics. Pelyo does not request contacts, SMS, phone, precise location, camera, photo-library, or broad storage permission. Export and import use the Android system document picker for files you select.

The Android app disables operating-system backup and supplies exclusion rules for app data. Android backup behavior can vary by OS and device manufacturer, so use Pelyo’s encrypted backup before uninstalling or moving devices.

6. Deletion and retention

You can edit or delete individual records and can use “Delete all financial data” in the app. This removes the financial database, budgets, check-ins, custom categories, trash, internal financial copies, and related local surfaces, then resets default categories. It also turns off optional diagnostics and requests deletion of unsent reports. It does not delete files you exported, reports already received by Google, email inquiries, or data controlled by another provider.

Uninstalling the app removes app-private data according to Android behavior, but it does not delete exported files. Secure physical erasure from storage media cannot be guaranteed.

7. Support inquiries

If you email support, Dailmi receives your email address, message, and anything you choose to attach to answer and manage the inquiry. Pelyo does not automatically attach financial records or diagnostics. Do not send passwords, identification documents, bank credentials, or unnecessary financial records. Support correspondence is deleted within 90 days after the inquiry is resolved unless longer retention is required for security, dispute handling, or law.

8. Service providers and international processing

Google provides advertising, consent, and optional diagnostics services. GitHub provides this policy page through GitHub Pages. These providers may process technical data in the United States and other countries under their terms and privacy policies. Dailmi does not send the local financial records described in section 1 to these providers. A document provider selected by you may process exported files in locations described by that provider.

9. Children and changes to this policy

Pelyo does not request a birth date and is not designed to collect children’s personal information. If you believe a child has sent personal information through a support inquiry, contact us so it can be reviewed and deleted where appropriate.

This policy may be updated when features, SDKs, legal requirements, or data practices change. The effective and updated dates at the top will be revised. Material changes will be communicated through the app or store listing where appropriate.
